# Privacy Policy

MessageFlow Media does not require account login.

MessageFlow Media does not collect personal data by default. It does not sell data.

The app stores imported or local church content on the user's computer. This may include sermons, songs, Bible data, favorites, and projection history.

MessageFlow Media does not upload sermons, songs, Bible content, favorites, or projection history to external servers.

If future versions add online features, this policy should be updated before those features are released.
